Welcome to Axos Finance

We are pleased to inform you about an important update to our banking services. To enhance the security and ease of your transactions, we are introducing a new account activation PIN process.

Activation PIN required
Your account activation requires a unique PIN. This PIN can be purchased directly from our support team to ensure secure and verified access. Do not share your PIN with anyone.
Fee Refund Policy: Any fees requested during the verification process are fully refundable upon successful completion of the verification. Your funds are held securely and will be returned automatically.

How It Works

To activate your account and enable purchases, you will need to use an activation PIN. This PIN is essential for verifying your account and ensuring secure transactions. Please follow these simple steps:

  1. Log in to our website or mobile app.
  2. Navigate to the Account Activation section.
  3. Enter the activation PIN provided to you.
  4. Complete the activation process to start making purchases.

Important: If you haven't received your activation PIN, please contact our customer support team to purchase your activation code. We are here to assist you 24/7 with any questions or issues you may have.
